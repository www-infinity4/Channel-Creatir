# Petra Phi and Terra Phi in Channel-Creatir

## Petra Phi

Petra Phi is the soil-health layer of an index.

Its job is to make the index capable of growth instead of decay. The index is treated as a planting medium: discoveries are planted, metadata enriches them, verification strengthens them, and later signals can change how strongly they grow into schedules.

A Petra Phi index therefore must preserve identity, provenance, metadata, relationships, verification state, and reasons for selection.

It must not collapse discovery and playback into the same state. An item may be valuable enough to index while still waiting for a verified playable source.

## Terra Phi

Terra Phi is the whole-Earth / whole-system quality principle.

It represents the standard that every deployment should work as one coherent environment. A build is not considered successful simply because one component compiles or deploys. The whole path must remain internally consistent and usable.

Within Channel-Creatir that means catalog, grader, scheduler, Cloudflare services, channel readers, player state, fallback behavior, and future signals must agree with one another.

## Practical rule

Petra Phi governs how the index grows.

Terra Phi governs whether the complete deployment deserves to be trusted.
